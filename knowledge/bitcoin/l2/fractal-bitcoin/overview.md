## What this covers

Fractal Bitcoin: a Bitcoin Core-derived scaling chain with ~30s blocks and Cadence Mining (part merge-mined with Bitcoin, part permissionless, part Index Mining since FIP-101). Mainnet 9 Sept 2024. FIP-102 / fractald v0.4.0 hard-forked at block 2,100,000, combining the first and second FB halvings; the chain passed that height before 15 September 2026.
